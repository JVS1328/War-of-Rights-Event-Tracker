General Conditions

**Factions**

- Each Faction will start with the following identical values:  
    
- Manpower (recruitment pool)  
- Money (used to buy men for tokens)  
- Cities (used to generate monthly manpower and money)  
- Tokens (The actual pieces controlled on the board)  
- Token Starting Strength (number of men in a token) 

**Balance**

- A Campaign will only go live when a suitable balance has been found that does not unfairly favour one faction.   
    
- Number of Actively controlled, that is controlled by a registered unit, tokens does not have to be identical, however preferably is to be as even as balance allows. Where there is a disparity in actively controlled tokens the additional tokens needed to achieve an even number will be labelled as Merc Tokens and are controlled as a group effort from the whole faction. 

**Turn Order**

- At the outset of a new Campaign all of the token tiles are placed in their relevant faction bags.   
    
- A coin will be tossed to decide which faction is drawn first, with Union being Heads and Confederates being Tails. The winning faction then draws from the tile bag first and that token holder places its token on the board. This continues until all tokens are on the board.  
    
- Following this the actual turns will begin, with the winner of the coin toss drawing from the bag first to make their move. Once all tokens have had their turn then the monthly manpower, money and event card is actioned the turn order will resume from where it left off, now from the alternate pair of bags.   
    
- If a token is engaged in combat when their tile is drawn, their turn is skipped and their board put in the discard bag. 

**Battle Win tracking**

- Victories will be tracked and tallied after every session on the Victories board on the tabletop  
    
- When a side achieves 5 victories they may draw a card from the Special Deck and will continue to do so every 5 victories.   
  ![][image2]

**Cards** 

- There are several decks of cards on the board, with differing uses:


Terrain Decks \- Used during the map selection phase to decide the maps which that week’s battles will be played on, each corresponding to terrain, forts and  cities on the map. For every Fields and Woods map a D20 will be rolled, if a 4 or under is rolled then the map will be drawn from the Conquest Deck. Once a map has been played it will be out for 2 weeks.

![][image3]s

Weather/Time \- Used to decide the weather and time during which a battle is fought. Keep in mind some cards from the Battle or Event decks may mean a weather or time card need not be drawn, for example “First Attack” which overrides the time deck and means a battle will be fought at Dawn.  
**![][image4]**

Battle Deck \- At the start of a new session, bar the very first session, 2 cards will be drawn face down from this deck for each battle that took place the previous session. The victor of that battle will then pick left or right and that card will apply to them, with the other card applying to the loser.   
![][image5]

Special Deck \- Once a faction has achieved 5 victories they will draw a single card from this deck. They will then draw another for every 5 victories. If a card from this deck affects a single token then the faction must decide amongst themselves whom it is allocated to.   
![][image6]

Event Deck \- At the start of every in game month, apart from the very first, a card will be drawn from this deck. If the card’s effect is immediate it will then be put in the discard pile. If the card’s effect lasts the whole month then it will sit on the calendar until the month has passed, then it will be added to the discard pile. Once all cards have been drawn you must then draw from the discard pile.   
![][image7]

**In Game Months/ Turns**

- In Game Months, also known as Turns are tracked using the board’s calendar. Every month the marker on the calendar will advance one month.

- A month passes once all of the tokens from each faction have been drawn from the current set of bags.   
    
- At the end of every month the month’s manpower and money gains must be added to both faction’s pools.   
    
- At the end of every month a card must be drawn from the Event Deck, this will trigger an event that will affect both sides. Be it immediately or throughout the entire month as stated on the card itself. 

- During winter months, tokens may still attack, but the attacking side will suffer 25% additional casualties due to attrition.

Tabletop

Layout

**Terrain**

- The terrain on the board is split into 3 varieties:  
  




-  The primary purpose of the differing terrains is to allow for a more variable and engaging map selection, with each terrain corresponding to a particular deck during the map selection period. Furthermore this allows the participants to engage in various levels of strategy based on which terrain they would rather defend or attack in.   
    
- With Field being by far the most abundant, the terrain a tile is classed as is decided by the presence of any of either the River or Forest terrains. If there is even so much as a pixel of either of these on the tile then the tile is classed as such. 


**Cities and Forts**

- Cities, marked by their owner faction’s colour, contribute monthly Manpower and Money gains to their owner faction.  
    
- During the map selection phase, if a battle is to take place over a city or fort then the map will be drawn from the Cities map deck. 

- Cities and Forts serve as locations for embarking on trains, much like stations which are tiles marked with the train symbol. Though a train can be disembarked anywhere it can only be embarked at one of these locations. 

- Cities and Forts serve as locations for replenishment also, with manpower only being able to be replenished in one of these locations.  
  **Map Selection**  
- Draw three cards from the relevant Map Deck and place them face down on the board. Draw a Time and a Weather card and place those face down.  
    
- Flip the 3 map cards. The defender decides which of the 3 they would least like to defend on and removes it, it goes back in the deck.  
    
- The attacker then decides from the remaining 2 which they would like to attack on. This will be where the battle is fought.

Economy

- Each side has an overall treasury. This starts at $8000, and can be increased by winning battles and taking cities and forts. The Base incoming is based on how many cities you have, each city grants a base income of 50$ per turn. 

- Taking a city or fort will get you $750   
    
- Winning a Battle will get you $400 

**Costs-**

- It costs $200 and 100 Manpower from the pool to replenish 100 Soldiers. You can only recruit in a city or fort.

Movement

   Rivers \- 3 Hexes per movement point.

   March \- 2 Hexes per movement point.

   Train \- 4 Hexes per movement point.

   

- Crossing Rivers: To cross a river you have to spend a movement point.

- Embarking and Disembarking a river ends your turn.

- Each turn, a token receives 2 movement points. Movement points do not stack between turns. A movement point ends when a new kind of  movement is started, e.g moving on land then joining a rail.,

- You can only board a train at a Fort, City, or Rail Station. Once you disembark, you must return to one of these locations to board another train.

   

- Train Combat: If attacked while on a train, your token will suffer an additional 15% casualties, similarly if you are travelling on a river.,

- A token can reinforce another in battle and take 40% of the casualties from the token that is engaged. 

- Disembarking from a Train ends your current turn.  Exception: If you play the Rails Card, you can continue your turn after disembarking from a train.

   

- Replenishing your token costs 1 movement point and ends your turn.

Garrisons 

- A maximum of 500  troops can be stationed in a city or fort as a garrison. When setting a garrison, the token's manpower is reduced by the number of stationed troops (up to 500). A token cannot add men to a Garrison if it brings them under 500 manpower. (Updated).

     
Garrison Combat:

- When a city or fort with a garrison is attacked, for every 100 garrison troops, they inflict 100 casualties on the attacking token. If there is a friendly token on the city then casualties will be taken from the garrison first in a battle. Placing a garrison or engaging one ends your turn.

Combat

- To declare an attack on an enemy token, it must be adjacent to yours, and you can only declare an attack during your turn.

- The attacker always plays the attacking side, regardless of the map setup.

- If a token loses a battle, it is pushed towards the nearest friendly city or fort up to 4 hexes, ignoring terrain penalties. If the retreat hex is occupied, the token will continue retreating to the next nearest city or fort.

- Entering an unoccupied enemy city or fort immediately ends your turn.

- You cannot attack from trains since jumping off a train ends your turn, you must jump off first to be able to attack next turn, unless you're driving into a city or have the New Rails card.

- If you are involved in a battle you gain one fatigue point, this stacks every battle you are in until your tile is drawn and you do not engage in combat, then your fatigue is reset to 0\. Each point of Fatigue increases your casualties by 5%. 

Conquest

- For every Fields and Woods map a D20 will be rolled, if a 4 or under is rolled then the map will be drawn from the Conquest Deck.

- When conquest is chosen, a coin is flipped to determine if factions should be swapped. Heads, teams play their respective factions on the board. Tails, teams play the opposite.  
    
- In the event that one side is victorious then the other will retreat 4 hexes, as with a standard skirmish win or loss.

- If the battle results in a draw then both sides retreat 2 hexes and split the winning money evenly. 

Last Stand

- When a token's manpower drops to between 100 and 500 men, it enters Last Stand status. If it goes under 100 men it is wiped without ever entering Last Stand.,

 **A token in Last Stand**:

- If a Last Stand Token is defeated in battle the enemy token will only receive up to double the manpower of the Last Stand token. (Up to meaning if the battle somehow results in less than double the manpower of the Last Stand token the enemy token will only receive the casualties as the appear in game)

- For example if X token is in Last Stand, having 400 men left and wins or Loses to Y token then Y token will only take 800 casualties. If Y only lost 700 men in-game then they would only lose 700\. 

**If victorious in battle, the Last Stand token:**

- Takes no casualties.  
    
- May retreat 4 hexes towards the nearest friendly City.  
    
- If the token loses a battle, it will be wiped from the game.  
    
- A token in Last Stand cannot capture cities or first, or engage in an attack on another token, this includes reinforcing an allied token. 

Win Conditions 

- Victory in Season 11 will again be determined by a points system. A team must accrue 10 Victory Points to claim victory. Capitals are worth 2 Victory Point and Wiping a token is worth 2 Victory Points. 

- CSA Capitals: Richmond, Wilmington, Atlanta

- USA Capitals: Washington, New York, Clevland

**General Rules:**

- Victory conditions are checked at the end of each in-game month (i.e., weekly after the battles take place). If either side achieves their win conditions, they are declared the winner. 

Mock Tabletop Session

**Before commencing**

- The Tabletop session is to be called Grand Campaign and the password shall be: odd

- Ensure you have loaded the correct, updated session

- Ensure a representative is present from each token holder. If one cannot be found or a stand-in has not been appointed then the factions affected must nominate someone to control that token for the session.

**First Steps**

- A quick recap on what went on the previous week and how the battles have affected the board

- Draw 2 cards from the Battle Deck for the first battle that has been fought the previous week. Lay then face down on the table. The victor of said battle gets to choose first. Then the loser gets the remaining card. Repeat this for the second battle. Apply any modifiers these cards enact based on how it is written on the card. If it is not an immediate effect then mark who it belongs to and place it under their Token Sheet.  
    
- If the previous session’s battles have resulted that one or both factions have achieved 5 victories. If so, draw them a single card from the Special Deck. If the card’s effect only applies to one token then the faction must decide amongst themselves who it applied to


**Turns**

- The token who last had a turn in the previous session’s tile should be sitting on the tile display board as a reminder. Place it in the respective discard bag and draw a tile from the opposing faction’s bag, they have the first turn of the session.  
    
- Ensure that if all tiles have been removed from a set of bags that you advance the calendar one month, add the manpower and money gains to the faction board, and draw a card from the Event Deck. All before continuing with the 2nd set of bags. 

- Continue drawing back and forth, using the cannon to keep track of which bag you are drawing from, until 2 battles have been declared.  
    
- Ensure to leave the last token who has played’s tile on the tile board so you know who’s turn it is next session.  
    
- If cities change hands be sure to update the Faction Sheets to reflect that one faction has gained a city and the other lost one. 

**Battles**

- First check if any of the engaged tokens have Battle or Special Cards that affect the battle in some way. Similarly check if that month’s Event Card will have an effect. Clearly lay these out near the Map Decks if so.   
    
- Set up one battle at a time, starting with the first that was engaged.  
    
- Check the terrain that the battle is being fought on, it is always the defender’s terrain that chooses which deck to draw from.  
    
- Draw three cards from the relevant Map Deck and place them face down on the board. Draw a Time and a Weather card and place those face down.  
    
- Flip the 3 map cards. The defender decides which of the 3 they would least like to defend on and removes it, it goes back in the deck.  
    
- The attacker then decides from the remaining 2 which they would like to attack on. This will be where the battle is fought.  
    
- Then turn over the Weather and Time cards.  
    
- Repeat for the second battle.


**Closing**

- Ensure balance is done and the battle info posted.   
    
- Quickly take any questions anyone may have  
    
- Ensure you have left the board tidy for the Battle Updater
